OneHR

Founder · Design & Build · 2025 – 2026

OneHR is a complete HR system for a small company, in one console: people, attendance, leave, payroll, expenses, assets, notices, approvals and the reports the accountant asks for. Twenty-eight modules, one login per person, and the same attendance figures flowing into payroll without a second sum. Everything the web console does, the phone app does too: the same modules, sized for a pocket, on Android and iOS.

I built OneHR alone, on Flutter for the web and the phone with Supabase behind it, every rule enforced in the database and walked by a QA account before a release. The cards below follow the onboarding manual, chapter by chapter: from signing in to the first one-click salary run.

WrapDB AI

Founder · Design & Build · 2026

WrapDB lets a business ask its own database a question in plain words and get the number, a chart, a table or a whole workbook back. On the web, on WhatsApp, or on Telegram. A small agent runs beside the customer’s database and only ever reads; the brain writes the SQL, checks it, and answers.

It reads what the database never had, too: Drive, Sheets, Excel and Jira. Built alone: Node brain, React chat, Claude for the language. Running in private beta with a live ERP of 1,281 tables behind it.

Spent

Founder · Design & Build · 2026

A note before the cards: Spent is not the best app I have made. OneHR, Harbor, Strekit and Blush above are bigger and better. It is here because it was my first Flutter app, and the first one I finished end to end, and I still use it every day. A personal expense tracker built around one idea: capturing a spend should take three taps, and one glance should tell you whether the month is on track. A pace ring does that job. Everything lives on the device; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

It also listened for GPay and PhonePe payment notifications and turned them into suggestions, so the ledger filled itself and you only confirmed. (Removed before release: the Play Store does not allow apps that read notifications.) Built alone in Flutter with SQLite underneath.
